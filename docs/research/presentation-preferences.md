# ADHD and autism presentation preferences

Otermans and colleagues, *How do adults with neurodevelopmental disorders prefer information being presented?* [Original PDF](https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/32230/4/FullText.pdf).

The survey included 204 people. Bullet instructions ranked highly across the groups. Heading emphasis and background choices varied. Favourite appearance did not always match ease of reading. Methods are on PDF pages 7–8; results are on pages 9–12.

The viewer uses short instructions and named groups. Appearance stays adjustable. These are reported preferences, not measured task results. The study has no separate combined ADHD/autism group. It supports choice rather than one required font or colour.
