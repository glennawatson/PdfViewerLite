# Print navigation

Klusewitz and Lorch, *Effects of headings and familiarity with a text on strategies for searching a text*, **Memory & Cognition** 28 (2000), 667–676. [Original PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/BF03201256.pdf). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03201256).

The study involved 128 students. Content headings helped readers inspect selected pages. Familiarity changed how they chose pages. Extra heading levels did not help every search task. See page 669 for methods and pages 673–675 for results.

The viewer names groups by their content. Stable preview positions are a separate app choice. The study did not test ADHD, autism, desktop controls or virtual print previews. It gives no page-flip timing formula.
