# Access guidance

W3C web guidance used as a reference for desktop controls:

| Guidance | App use |
|---|---|
| [Visible labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o4p06-clear-labels/) and [form labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/labels/) | Familiar words and accessible names |
| [Familiar design](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o1p02-familiar-design/) | Predictable groups and routes |
| [Controls and content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o1p06-control-actions/) | Keep actions near what they affect |
| [Alternatives to dragging](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements) | Keyboard and click alternatives |

This guides the design. It is not a claim of WCAG certification.
