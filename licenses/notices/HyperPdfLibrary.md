### Predefined CMaps and CID-to-Unicode tables (Adobe cmap-resources)
- Origin: Bundled with the app
- Copyright: Copyright 1990-2009 Adobe Systems Incorporated. Copyright 2014 The PDFium Authors.
- Link: https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources

````text
The *.bin files in this folder are predefined CJK CMaps and CID-to-Unicode tables, packed for HyperPdfLibrary.

Most tables are converted from PDFium's core/fpdfapi/cmaps (https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium), which PDFium
derived from Adobe's cmap-resources. The codespace ranges of every CMap and the Uni*-UTF32-H/V CMaps are converted
from Adobe's cmap-resources as compiled by pdf.js (external/bcmaps). Both sources are under the licences below.
````
